How a new version replaces the old one

A new version is released beside the old one, never in its place. Products then have a 90-day notice period to re-run their acceptance test and move before the old version retires.

  • Step in the release
  • Trigger and gap diagnosis
  • Serving as current
  • Dev only, or handled by a further version
  • Deprecated, or stops here
