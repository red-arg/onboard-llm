We are designing for a low volume platform. Expect only 0 to 2 projects onboarding onto the platform per month.
Do not design around exceptions, standardization matters more than having exceptions for designing frameworks.
We only have five roles: Business Owner (owns the project), Developers (develop the project), Platform Owner, Production Engineers (platform engineers) and the Platform data science team. Do not add any other role, such as a review board, Security, Legal, data governance or SRE.
The LLMs are hosted in an environment that is approved for data up to a certain classification across the board. Every model, archetype and product on the platform shares that one ceiling, so do not design per-model, per-archetype or per-product data classification levels, gates, fields or approvals.
